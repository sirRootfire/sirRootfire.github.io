About Me

A security researcher who thinks like an attacker to protect your business.

Automated scanners often look for known signatures while completely missing critical logical vulnerabilities. I focus on finding flaws that impact your direct revenue and customer privacy—specifically Broken Object Level Authorization (BOLA/IDOR), authentication bypasses, and privilege escalations.

When you partner with me, you don't receive an overwhelming and generic 200-page scanner dump. Instead, you get concise, step-by-step reproduction steps (PoC), clear business impact analysis, and practical remediation guidance tailored for your developers.
