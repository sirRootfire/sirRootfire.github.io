About Me

I am Khaled Gamal Seif (@sirrootfire), a penetration tester and security researcher. Automated tools often scan for surface-level signatures while missing deep architectural flaws. My approach focuses on practical impact and adversary simulation—breaking perimeters the way real attackers do so you can remediate them before a breach occurs.

My offensive assessments span web applications (with an emphasis on broken access control, IDOR, and business logic bypasses), internal network penetration, and Active Directory attack paths such as domain privilege escalation and lateral movement.

By combining offensive tooling (Burp Suite, BloodHound, Impacket) with custom Python/Bash scripting and solid Linux fundamentals, I ensure you receive actionable, proof-of-concept-backed reports rather than generic scanner noise.
